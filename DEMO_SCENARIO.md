# Kich ban demo nhanh

Muc tieu: cho thay thay duoc day du cac tinh nang chinh cua Smart Coffee Shop trong 5-10 phut.

## 1. Dang nhap
- Dang nhap voi tai khoan admin.
- Gioi thieu nhanh: he thong quan ly don hang, nhan vien, lich phan ca, cham cong, thong ke.

## 2. Dashboard ban hang
- Mo trang dashboard don hang.
- Loc theo trang thai `pending`, `preparing`, `completed`.
- Mo 1 don hang dang xu ly.
- Chuyen trang thai sang `completed`.
- Neu co diem tich luy, chi ra phan cap nhat diem khach hang.

## 3. Quan ly nhan vien
- Mo danh sach nhan vien.
- Vao 1 nhan vien dang lam.
- Sua thong tin co ban hoac luong/gio.
- Bo tick `Dang lam`.
- Luu lai.
- Chi ra rang cac phan ca trong tuong lai cua nhan vien do da bi xoa, con lich qua khu van giu nguyen.

## 4. Lich phan ca
- Mo `Lich phan ca nhan vien`.
- Chon 1 ngay trong thang 6.
- Them 1 phan ca moi.
- Sua 1 phan ca da co.
- Xoa 1 phan ca.
- Demo bo loc nhan vien neu can.
- Chi ra canh bao du/thieu nhan su tren lich.

## 5. Cham cong
- Mo danh sach cham cong.
- Loc theo thang 6.
- Chi ra du lieu cham cong da duoc seed san de test tong gio lam.

## 6. Tinh luong / bao cao
- Mo trang tong hop luong theo thang.
- Chon thang 6.
- Chi ra tong so ca, tong gio, luong uoc tinh.

## 7. Tong ket
- Neu thay hoi ve tinh nang xoa phan ca:
  - Neu nhan vien ngung lam, he thong chi xoa lich phan ca tu ngay hien tai tro di.
  - Du lieu lich su va cham cong qua khu khong bi tac dong.

## Diem can nhan manh khi bao cao
- Co phan quan ly nhan vien lien ket voi tai khoan dang nhap.
- Co lich phan ca truc quan, co canh bao du/thieu nguoi.
- Co cham cong va tong hop luong theo thang.
- Du lieu demo da duoc seed dam dac trong thang 6 de test day du.
